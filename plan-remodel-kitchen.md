# Kitchen Remodel Planning Document

## 1.0 Overview

**Description:**

Complete kitchen remodel and rennovation

**High Level List:**

1. Replace oven, vent-hood, and dish washer
2. Remove tile and replace with laminite wood flooring
3. Remove wainescot
4. Remove soffit bulkhead above existing cabinets
5. New cabinets
6. New Counter top
7. New kitchen sink
8. Repair drywall
9. New recessed light fixtures
10. New electrical zone for kitchen from breaker
11. New backsplash solution
12. Paint

## 2.0 Overhead

*   Permits: Check with local building department for required permits for electrical and plumbing work.
*   Dumpster Rental: 10-yard dumpster for debris.
*   Tools: Purchase or rent any necessary tools not already owned.

## 6.0 Procedures

The project will begin with the complete demolition of the existing kitchen. This will be followed by the rough-in of new electrical and plumbing. Once the drywall is repaired, the new flooring will be installed. Cabinets and countertops will be installed next, followed by the appliances. The final steps will be the installation of the backsplash, painting, and installation of the new light fixtures.

## 8.0 Cost / Benefit

*   **Added Value / Cost:** A kitchen remodel is estimated to recoup 60-80% of its cost in added home value. For an $8,450 investment, this is an estimated value increase of $5,070 - $6,760.
*   **Added Value / Time:** The project will provide a more functional and aesthetically pleasing kitchen, improving daily life.

## 9.0 Considerations

*   We will need to set up a temporary kitchen in the dining room for the duration of the remodel.
*   We will need to be mindful of dust and debris and take steps to protect the rest of the house.
*   We should consider doing the work in the spring or fall when the weather is mild.
